# Code of Conduct

BrainGate adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions,
and any other channel where someone represents the project.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, give and take feedback on the work rather than the
person, and do not harass anyone. The full text linked above is the one that applies.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers at the contact listed in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers
may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or
permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they judge harmful, following the enforcement guidelines of
the Contributor Covenant.
